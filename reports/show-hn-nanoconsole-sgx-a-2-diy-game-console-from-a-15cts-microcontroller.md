The NanoConsole The nanoconsole SGX is a small, cheap DIY video game console with an integrated sound system and controller. Its core is a tiny 15-cent RISC-V processor with 16k of Flash, with no dedicated video or USB hardware. However, it (barely) drives a VGA screen with colors, (somewhat) manages to have a USB bootloader, and even runs games. The software library is based on the ch32fun and rv003usb projects Specs CPU: ch32v003 RISC-V SOP16 (15-cent) - 48MHz 16k Flash, 2k RAM (RAM is expensive, okay?) Video: 320x240 VGA, 4 palettes of 2 colors swappable by line (GPU: none :) (black/green, purple/white, blue/cyan, red/yellow) Sound: integrated buzzer, 1 channel square wave (also named "beeps") Controller: 4 buttons on the board itself: Left/Right, A/B Connectivity: VGA, USB (power and game uploads), no network Community Contribute to the repository of the project.
