Date: Thu, 12 Dec 1996 14:36:52 GMT From: James Souttar Subject: Re: Euro symbol The front page of the European this week (12-18 December 1996) shows a proposed design for the 'euro' symbol (i.e. the currency symbol for our proposed European single currency). The article describes it as a 'post modern pretzel' [I wasn't aware that pretzels came out of the modern movement, but you learn something every day &lt;g&gt;] It is a monoline three quarters circular arc, the open quarter to the right, with the ends cut off at an angle (the top sharply angled like Syntax, the bottom straight like Gill), and two horizontal bars cutting through arc, their ends both angled.
