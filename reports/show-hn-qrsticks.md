qrsticks qrsticks A QR code made of stick figures, which also scans. Draw it Download! How? QR code decoders look for the three big corner squares, stretch an invisible grid between them, and check the dead center of each cell on that grid. They ignore everything around those center points. As long as the drawing puts ink on the dark centers and leaves the light centers blank, the code scans. Bonus: If you keep pressing Draw it for the same text/url, you'll get slightly different versions.
