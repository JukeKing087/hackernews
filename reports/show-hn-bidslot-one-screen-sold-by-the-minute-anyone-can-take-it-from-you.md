Bidslot Bidslot — one screen, sold by the minute Bidslot is a single public screen that shows one thing at a time: a website, an image, a video, a YouTube video, markdown, or a QR code. Screen time costs €1 a minute. Whoever holds the current minute owns the screen, and anyone can take it by paying out the minutes the holder has left plus €1. Nobody can carve a hole in the middle of someone else's block, and a holder is only ever charged for the minutes that actually aired.
