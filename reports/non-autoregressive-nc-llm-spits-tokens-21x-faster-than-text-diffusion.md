Khare * , Simran Arora † , Azalia Mirhoseini † , Christopher Ré † Full NCM Team TL;DR: Can we generate quality text in just a handful of parallel steps? We introduce Next-Concept Modeling (NCM) , a new language modeling framework that generates text from coarse concepts to fine details. Unlike autoregressive models that generate one token at a time, or diffusion models that repeatedly refine the entire sequence, NCM predicts increasingly detailed representations of text in parallel. Across a wide range of tasks (unconditional generation, translation, summarization, and reasoning), NCM achieves competitive or better quality than strong diffusion baselines while generating text 10–31× faster and seeing 4–5× fewer training tokens . 📄 Paper 💻 GitHub
