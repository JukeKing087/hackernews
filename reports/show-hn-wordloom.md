Wordloom Wordloom v0.9 By 🦉 William Godfrey, billpg.com . (and Claude ) Drag the tiles so every row and column spells a real word. New game every day at ! How to play Metal tiles are fixed in place. Drag the stone tiles from the tray into the empty slots, or tap a tile to lift it and tap again on where it should go. You win once every row and every column spells a real word. The dot beside each row and below each column is a hint: grey means nothing's been placed there yet, amber means a word could still work, red means nothing on the list can. Green means the word is valid, but not necessarily the one you need to finish the game, because every word has to be valid in both directions at once. Flip swaps the grid's rows and columns, keeping everything you've placed so far. Shuffle reorders the tray - nothing on the board moves, it just gives the leftover tiles a new arrangement to look at.
