Produced in partnership with research firm Engage R+D , the study draws on a survey of 336 independent, community-led archives, and in-depth interviews with more than 20 practitioners across the country. Community-based archives are independent organizations that collect and preserve the records, photographs, oral histories, personal papers, and other artifacts of communities many of which have been historically excluded from mainstream institutions.
