Scott Stevenson Aug 05, 2025 17 Share Modularity is seductive in product development: Rather than doing one thing, give the user a system of components—ingredients. Allow them to combine those ingredients to create the workflow they need. Kill 50 birds with 1 system. I tried this a lot in my early journey as an engineer, and it failed 90% of the time. Now I believe that this tendency—let’s call it Modularity Bias —is one of the most common risks for early stage product teams. Phonebloks promised a future where we could assemble phones out of modular components to meet our needs—and upgrade them over time. It did not succeed.
