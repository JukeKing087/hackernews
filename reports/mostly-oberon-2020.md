Doiel, 2020-04-11 Mostly Oberon is a series of blog posts documenting my exploration of the Oberon Language, Oberon System and the various rabbit wholes I inevitably fell into. Overview Oberon is a classical computer language and operating system originated by Professors Niklaus Wirth and Jürg Gutknecht at ETH circa 1987. It was inspired by their experiences in California at the Xerox Palo Alto Research Center . This series of blog posts are my meandering exploration of Oberon-07 language based on Project Oberon 2013 . NOTE: Oberon grew from Wirth's Modula, which grew from Pascal, which grew from his experiences with Algol. My Voyage I am new to both Oberon and the Oberon System.
