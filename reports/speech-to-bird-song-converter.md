This site turns human voice into birdsong. It's inspired by windytan's Blackbird experiment How does it work? A voice is a stack of harmonics over a fundamental pitch. The converter shifts that fundamental down to near 0 Hz, low-pass filters it to isolate it, and then uses FM demodulation to track its instantaneous frequency. AM demodulation on the same signal gives the amplitude envelope. The original harmonics are thrown away.
