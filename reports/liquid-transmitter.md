A wire connected to the diaphragm of the transmitter extends into the copper cup filled with vinegar and just barely makes contact with the liquid.&nbsp; As the diaphragm vibrates, the contact area varies and with it the resistance.&nbsp; The signal can be sent to the speaker/amplifier or recorded and played back.&nbsp; The audio quality is not good but spoken words are discernible.
