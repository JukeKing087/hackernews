Friction as a Feature My new course called aiforui.dev is in early access! Early access has closed. Emil Kowalski Design Engineer We treat friction as something bad. It makes us slow, inefficient, messy. Products brag about removing friction. Entire companies exist just to be “frictionless”. And mostly, that’s right. But friction was quietly doing a second job: it made us think before we built. Before AI, writing code was expensive, so the cost of building imposed a higher threshold on which ideas survived long enough to be built. You had to be selective because the effort itself forced you to decide what was worth making. AI removed that cost. Now an idea can become an app in minutes. So the bar for what’s worth building lowered drastically. To be fair, cheap building is a way of thinking.
